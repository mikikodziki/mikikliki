n°002 · Craft

What I deliver

A system your team can navigate on day one, in Figma and in code.

01 Foundations
Audits, design tokens and accessibility baked in from the first primitive.
02 Component libraries
Guidelines, atoms, molecules, organisms: every variant, size and state accounted for.
03 Modes & theming
Light, dark and brand themes, localisation-ready. Built into foundations, not bolted on.
04 Documentation
Usage rules, do & don't, consistent naming. If it isn't documented, it isn't done.
05 Product craft
Screens that use the system well, with the polish that makes a product feel considered.
